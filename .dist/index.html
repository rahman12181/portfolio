YourHome (Nestora)

Find a place that feels like home.

A student accommodation and property platform. Tenants discover PGs, rooms and flats, owners manage and promote their listings, and admins track the whole business. One backend, a Flutter app and a Next.js website, working together.

In active development Flutter, Spring Boot, Next.js

  • 150+Secure REST APIs

The problem
Students and working people looking for accommodation search across scattered sources, with little trust and no easy way to compare. Owners lack a simple way to present, promote and manage their listings.
The solution
One connected product with separate experiences for tenants, owners and admins: search with maps, rich photo and video listings, real-time chat, and paid plans that give owners visibility and tenants access.
My contribution
Everything: product thinking, the Spring Boot backend, the Flutter app, the Next.js web frontend, payments and integrations. A solo-founder build across the full stack.

Engineering approach

How I build.

  1. Understand the requirement

    Start from the workflow and who relies on it. For attendance, that means what counts as a valid check-in before any screen is drawn.

  2. Design reliable mobile experiences

    Plan for poor connectivity, denied permissions and slow responses, because real users meet them daily.

  3. Integrate APIs and data

    Keep the client and backend contract explicit, and keep data handling in one place rather than scattered through screens.

  4. Handle authentication and errors

    Treat sign-in, expired sessions and failed requests as first-class states, with clear messages instead of silent failures.

  5. Write maintainable code

    Separate UI, state and data access, and use consistent naming so the next person can change it safely.

  6. Test and improve

    Check behaviour on real devices, fix what breaks, and tune what feels slow.
